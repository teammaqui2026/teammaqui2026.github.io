Reminders & FAQs

What time should we arrive? +
Our ceremony starts promptly at 2:00 PM. We recommend arriving 30 to 45 minutes earlier to allow ample time for comfortable parking and church seating.
Can I bring a plus-one or children? +
Due to venue capacity and guest list limitations, our celebration is strictly an intimate affair. We can only accommodate the specific seats reserved for you in your invitation RSVP.
Is there parking available? +
Yes! Designated parking spaces are available at both The Fairview Park United Methodist Church and Blue Gardens Events Venue.
Can we take photos during the reception? +
Yes, absolutely! Feel free to capture all the joyful memories and celebrate together with us during cocktail hour and the reception.
